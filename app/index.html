Pair your device

Three steps, once. Your phone keeps the signing key; the relay only carries the message.

  1. 1

    Join the device Wi-Fi

    Power the device (or hold BOOT for 5 s) and join the remote-wake-xxxx network. The password is printed on the device.

  2. 2

    Give it this phone's public key

    Open http://192.168.4.1, enter your home Wi-Fi and relay URL, and paste this key:

  3. 3

    Paste what it gives back

    The setup page returns a small JSON blob. Paste it here.

Pairing only works in the device's setup mode — never over your home network, never through the relay.

Security

Unlock text

The relay sees this text. In v1 the type action's arguments travel in the clear to the relay (they are signed, not encrypted). It is stored only on this phone, but prefer a short PIN login and run your own relay.
